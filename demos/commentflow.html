From simulator to a real page — the safe way

  1. Design + test here first. Run your flow in the simulator until the DM conversation feels human — short lines, one question at a time, easy exit.
  2. Connect through an official tool only — GoHighLevel workflows or ManyChat, both Meta-approved. Never automate a personal profile; that is what gets accounts banned.
  3. Recreate the flow there using the exact texts from your Studio (use “Copy flow as JSON” to carry everything over).
  4. Keep the ban-safe habits: rotate at least 3 public replies, add a small delay before the DM, one keyword per post, no public links in the caption (the link lives in the DM).
  5. Send every captured lead to WhatsApp — the Leads tab gives you a one-tap WhatsApp follow-up with a pre-written message, because DMs go cold and WhatsApp closes.

Why a studio first? A broken flow on a real page burns your one viral post. A tested flow turns it into customers. CommentFlow is the rehearsal room; GHL/ManyChat is the stage.
